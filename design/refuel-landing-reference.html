Signing is free. 3 refuels per wallet, one every 24 hours. Testnet only.

One signature, no gas

  1. 1

    You sign a permit

    An EIP-2612 signature lets Refuel take the MUSD amount you picked. It's a signature, not a transaction, so it costs nothing.

  2. 2

    The relayer sends it

    Our relayer submits refuelWithPermit and pays the gas. It can't change where the BTC goes: only to the wallet that signed.

  3. 3

    BTC lands in your wallet

    The contract prices BTC with Mezo's PriceFeed and sends native BTC. Your next transaction pays its own gas.

Built on Mezo's own contracts

What it touches

Refuel reads Mezo's PriceFeed, pulls MUSD through its built-in permit, and pays out native BTC. The contract is verified, so you can read every line.
